# 💫 About Me:
What I do in my current job as a sustainable material manager at Jansen Recycling, I am responsible for the management, coordination, and quality assurance of incoming and outgoing material streams.<br>This includes:<br>• Coordination of material streams: I manage the supply and removal of raw materials as well as construction and demolition waste.<br>• Quality control: I monitor and ensure the quality and verify the certificates of incoming materials and recycled outgoing products.<br>• Laboratory analyses: I am responsible for managing lab requests and analyses and interpret the results.<br>• Specifications and reporting: I analyze project specifications and maintain clear, organized reports on raw material streams.<br>• Collaboration: I work in close coordination with various stakeholders across operations, the transport department, different contractors and environmental authorities<br><br>competences and programs<br>Extensive experience with software including Excel, ArcGIS, QGIS, TerraIndex, Street View, GaBi, PowerPoint, and Nuance PDF;<br>• Knowledge of relevant legislation and regulations (the Environment and Planning Act [environmental laws], BRL and NEN laws, and SIKB protocols);<br>• Fluent in English, partly due to my English-language degree program, ESSET;<br>• Sociable, eager to learn, and resilient under pressure.
